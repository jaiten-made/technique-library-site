Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026.

What the app stores

Technique Library stores the techniques you save (YouTube video links, titles, start times, key details), your Focus list and your sparring records. It keeps them on your device and, if you connect Google Drive, in a hidden app data folder in your own Google Drive. There is no server: the developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks for access to its own app data folder only (drive.appdata). It cannot see any other file in your Drive. It uses this access only to keep your devices in sync.

YouTube access

If you import a YouTube playlist, the app asks for read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) for that import. It reads only the video IDs in the playlist you name, and keeps no sign-in afterwards. Titles and thumbnails are looked up from YouTube's public video details. By using the app's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

The app shares nothing with anyone. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It only contacts Google sign-in, Google Drive and YouTube, to do the things above.

Technique Library's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your device. The Drive copy can be deleted from Google Drive's settings under Manage apps.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/jaiten-made/technique-library-site.
